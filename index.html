What we're building

The first version of TrainSight AI focuses on making training evaluation data easier to analyze and understand.

📊

Training analytics

Explore instructor performance, course evaluations, ratings, trends, and other training-related metrics.

💬

Feedback summarization

Use AI to summarize participant comments and identify common themes from qualitative feedback.

🔎

Natural-language exploration

Ask questions about training data without manually creating every filter or analysis.

✨

AI-assisted insights

Use Claude to help generate concise summaries and surface patterns that can support further analysis.

How it works

The planned workflow is intentionally simple for the first prototype.

STEP 01

Upload data

Users provide structured training evaluation data and participant feedback.

STEP 02

Analyze

The platform processes key metrics and prepares relevant context for AI-assisted analysis.

STEP 03

Ask & explore

Users can ask questions and receive summaries based on the available training data.

Currently in development

TrainSight AI is an early prototype. Initial development is using synthetic data while the product workflow and Claude integration are being tested.
